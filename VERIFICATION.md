my own machine before submitting: macOS (Apple Silicon), Go 1.27.3, PostgreSQL 16.11 (Homebrew), on a
fresh clone and an empty database.

## Independent checks
- **GST residual:** `sql/queries/gst_residual_records.sql` on the after-fix run. 805 failing records,
  1,610 record-fields. The expected GST residual computed from the raw settlement rows is also 805 /
  1,610, with 0 mismatching record-fields.
- **Deferred tie-out**, queried from `source_rows`: all in-scope payment records 121,902.51 − Deferred
  43,540.07 + payout transfer 133,756.51 = 212,118.95.
- **Raw-file control total**, outside the pipeline: summing the `amount` column of
  `data/amazon_settlements_data.txt` with `awk` gives 212,118.95, equal to the header `total-amount`.

The reports in `reports/` and `db/recon_dump.sql.gz` in this commit were regenerated by these runs.
